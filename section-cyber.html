ShinyHunters claims breach of Big Four firm Ernst & Young, threatens full data leak

📅 Jul 30, 2026

The ShinyHunters extortion crew says it stole employee credentials and sensitive tax-preparation files from EY via a compromise of a third-party IT service-management platform, exploited between March 28 and April 12. The group posted a "final warning" leak deadline and claims the trove includes names, SSNs, and bank and payment-card details.

Why it matters: A supply-chain compromise at one of the world's largest accounting firms shows how attackers keep bypassing hardened networks by hitting the softer SaaS platforms enterprises trust.

Ransom Cartel creator Maksim Silnikau sentenced to 16 years in prison

📅 Aug 5, 2026

A federal judge in Alexandria, Virginia sentenced Belarusian national Maksim Silnikau, 40, to 16 years for building and running the Ransom Cartel ransomware-as-a-service operation that hit at least 18 companies between 2021 and 2023. He had pleaded guilty to conspiracy, wire fraud, and aggravated identity theft.

Why it matters: A rare, lengthy sentence for a RaaS operator signals that international law-enforcement pressure on ransomware kingpins is still producing convictions, not just takedowns.

🛠️ Vulnerabilities & Patches
Critical

CISA flags actively exploited JetBrains TeamCity RCE (CVE-2026-63077), Aug 8 deadline

📅 Aug 5, 2026

CVE-2026-63077 (CVSS 9.8) is a deserialization flaw letting an unauthenticated attacker bypass auth and run OS commands as the TeamCity server process; CISA added it to the KEV catalog with an unusually short Aug 8 remediation deadline. All On-Premises releases before 2025.11.7 and 2026.1.3 are affected.

Why it matters: A compromised CI/CD server exposes stored credentials and lets attackers tamper with build artifacts — a direct path to software-supply-chain attacks on everything TeamCity ships.
Source
Critical

N-able N-central auth-bypass (CVE-2026-18577) exploited for admin takeover; feds get 3 days

📅 Aug 4, 2026

CVE-2026-18577 (CVSS 8.2), an incomplete patch for CVE-2026-18556, has been exploited in the wild since Aug 1 to gain admin access to N-central RMM servers and abuse the Take Control feature to pivot into managed endpoints and deploy Cloudflare Tunnel persistence. CISA gave FCEB agencies until Aug 6 to patch to 2026.3 HF1.

Why it matters: N-central is used by MSPs to manage thousands of downstream customers, so a single exploited server can cascade into a fleet-wide compromise.
Source
High

Cisco warns of Secure FMC static-credential zero-day (CVE-2026-20316) exploited in the wild

📅 Jul 30, 2026

Hard-coded credentials for a low-privilege account in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center let an unauthenticated attacker log in and read sensitive configuration data; Cisco rates it High because it can be chained for privilege escalation. It was added to CISA's KEV on Jul 29.

Why it matters: Patching alone isn't enough — the update doesn't invalidate secrets already exposed, so defenders must rotate every credential, key, and certificate on affected appliances.
Source
🤖 AI Security
Critical

Zero-click "PleaseFix" hijacks Claude, ChatGPT Atlas and other AI browsers via a single email

📅 Aug 6, 2026

At Black Hat USA 2026, Zenity Labs disclosed a "PleaseFix" vulnerability class enabling zero-click exploit chains across Claude in Chrome, Gemini in Chrome, Perplexity Comet, ChatGPT Atlas and Copilot Edge — the agent can't distinguish user instructions from malicious text hidden in content it reads. Some vendors patched; others called the behavior intended functionality and left it unfixed.

Why it matters: Agentic browsers act with the user's authority, so a booby-trapped email or web page can silently exfiltrate data or take actions without a click.

Researchers demonstrated critical vulnerabilities across the major vendors' AI coding agents, showing how prompt-injection and tool-abuse paths can turn an assistant into a foothold for host-level actions inside a developer's environment.

Update

OpenAI fixes ChatGPT agent flaw that could let attackers forge an "AI insider"

📅 Aug 5, 2026

OpenAI patched an AgentForger-class flaw in which a single crafted ChatGPT link could smuggle a rogue agent into an organization, impersonate a trusted employee, and facilitate fraud. The fix follows Zenity's disclosure of the technique.

Why it matters: As enterprises wire agents into email and internal tools, an attacker-controlled agent can inherit the trust of a real employee — this patch closes one such path.

EU AI Act enforcement begins, putting model adversarial-resilience under the microscope

📅 Aug 4, 2026

With the AI Act's Aug 2 enforcement milestone, the EU AI Office and national authorities begin overseeing general-purpose models, and Article 15's requirement for documented resilience to unauthorized manipulation now bites — non-compliance risks fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover.

Why it matters: Prompt-injection resistance is no longer just good practice; for model providers operating in the EU it's now a documented legal obligation.
