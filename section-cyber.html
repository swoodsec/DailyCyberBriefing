500+ Victims

CISA: Medusa ransomware hit over 500 critical infrastructure orgs

📅 Aug 19, 2026

An updated joint advisory from CISA, the FBI and HHS says Medusa's RaaS affiliates have breached 500+ organizations since 2021 — concentrated in healthcare, defense industrial base, critical manufacturing and government — using an affiliate model where victims get 48 hours before Medusa contacts them directly and starts a leak-site countdown timer.

Why it matters: Patch internet-facing systems and segment networks now — Medusa affiliates are still active and the 48-hour negotiation window leaves little time to react once encryption starts.

3.64M Records

Hacker claims 3.6 million Azure account records stolen from major companies

📅 Aug 17, 2026 (updated Aug 19)

A threat actor known as "TheHatman" has posted Azure/Entra employee-directory dumps for nine Fortune 500 firms — McDonald's (1.7M records), TCS (800K), Vodafone (425K), HCL, IHG, Kyndryl, Gap, Hexaware and Wyndham — claiming compromised credentials and MFA fatigue as the access vector. Hudson Rock linked several victims to prior infostealer infections; TCS, Gap and Vodafone say the data is old and non-sensitive.

Why it matters: Whether or not the data is "old," accurate org charts and manager/report details are gold for spear-phishing — audit infostealer-log exposure for your domain and tighten Conditional Access/CAE enforcement on Entra tenants.

Active Threat Advisory

US warns of AI-powered attacks on Siemens PLCs in critical infrastructure

📅 Aug 19, 2026

NSA, CISA, FBI, DOE and EPA say attackers are using Censys and ZoomEye to find internet-exposed Siemens S7-200 through S7-1500 PLCs, then using AI to write Python exploitation scripts (via snap7.dll/python-snap7) disguised as OT monitoring software to read and write PLC memory and ladder logic over S7comm. Activity spans Critical Manufacturing, Energy, Water, Chemical, Food/Ag and Commercial Facilities sectors and appears focused on reconnaissance for now.

Why it matters: This follows the July Minnesota water-utility PLC attacks — inventory every Siemens S7 device, take them off the public internet, and don't assume "just reconnaissance" stays that way.

📅 Aug 19, 2026

A previously unreported, medium-confidence China-nexus cluster is hitting government ministries across Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Kazakhstan with spear-phishing lures and seven RAT families — five never documented before (DriveSilkRAT, CookiETagRAT, NomadRAT, GoginRAT, NodeEdgeRAT) — alongside BLOODALCHEMY, a ShadowPad-derived backdoor. Researchers found clear traces of AI-assisted development woven into otherwise expert-level tooling.

Law Enforcement

US charges Iranian hackers over $3.4 billion intellectual property theft

📅 Aug 19, 2026

DOJ unsealed a 14-count superseding indictment against 17 alleged Mabna Institute members for a 2013–2017 campaign that hit 144 U.S. and 178 foreign universities, 42 U.S. companies, five federal/state agencies and two NGOs on behalf of Iran's IRGC, stealing roughly 31.5TB of academic data and IP. The DOJ is offering up to $10 million each for five of the defendants still at large.

Why it matters: A reminder that credential-phishing campaigns against universities and researchers can have years-long intelligence payoffs — academic IT and library-resource portals remain a soft target worth hardening.

New Malware

New Manic Android malware can exfiltrate data through nearby devices

📅 Aug 20, 2026

Active since February and primarily targeting Ukraine, Manic combines spyware, banking fraud and remote control across 169 banking/government-eID/crypto/2FA apps, using Accessibility-service overlays as a UI keylogger. When it can't reach its C2 server it falls back to relaying encrypted data through nearby infected devices over Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth, up to four hops — meaning even offline phones can leak data.

Why it matters: Deny Accessibility permissions to apps that don't need them and avoid sideloaded APKs — this malware is built to keep exfiltrating even when you think you've cut off its network access.

CVSS 10.0

Microsoft Entra ID Flaw (CVSS 10.0) Exploited in Wild, Allows Remote Code Execution

📅 Aug 21, 2026

CVE-2026-69836 is a deserialization-of-untrusted-data RCE in Entra ID (formerly Azure AD) that Microsoft confirms was exploited in the wild. Because Entra ID is a cloud service, Microsoft says it has already fully mitigated the flaw server-side and no customer action is required — details on how it was exploited or discovered haven't been released.

Why it matters: No patch to deploy here, but a maximum-severity RCE against your identity provider being exploited before public disclosure is a strong reason to review Entra sign-in and audit logs from the past month for anomalies.

Cloud Credential Theft

CISA warns of hackers exploiting critical MLflow vulnerability

📅 Aug 20, 2026

CVE-2026-64849 is a DNS-rebinding SSRF bypass in MLflow's unauthenticated webhook-test endpoint, patched in 3.15.0, that lets attackers make the tracking server fetch AWS/GCP/Azure cloud-metadata endpoints and read the response — handing over IAM credentials. Honeypots have already captured real exploitation, with attackers deploying cryptominers and creating rogue IAM users after credential theft. CISA added it to the KEV catalog and gave federal agencies two weeks to patch.

Why it matters: MLflow has 30M+ monthly downloads and is often deployed with the default no-auth SQLite config — audit exposed tracking-server instances and upgrade to 3.15.0+ immediately.

Critical Zimbra RCE flaw now actively exploited in attacks

📅 Aug 20, 2026

CERT Polska confirms attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-73570, an unauthenticated command-injection RCE in Zimbra Collaboration Suite's SNMP monitoring component when notifications are enabled, patched July 20 in version 10.1.20. Shadowserver counts over 12,100 internet-exposed Zimbra servers, concentrated in Europe and Asia.

Why it matters: Zimbra is a recurring target for state-linked actors (APT29, APT28) — patch to 10.1.20+ now and check logs per CERT Polska's guidance for unexpected service restarts or files dropped in Zimbra's webapps/tmp directories.

CISA KEV Update

Critical RCE flaw in Windows IKE Extension now actively exploited

📅 Aug 18, 2026

CISA added CVE-2026-33824, a pre-auth double-free RCE in Windows IKE Service Extensions reachable via crafted UDP packets on ports 500/4500, to its KEV catalog after confirming active exploitation. Organizations that can't patch immediately should block inbound UDP 500/4500 or restrict IKE traffic to known peer addresses.

Why it matters: This affects every supported Windows 10/11/Server release and needs no authentication — treat it as a priority patch alongside this week's Entra ID and MLflow advisories.

🤖 AI Security
One-Click Data Theft

Microsoft Copilot Personal Flaws Could Let One Click Exfiltrate Data From Connected Apps

📅 Aug 18, 2026

Varonis's "CoSnitch" research (CVE-2026-24301) found that pairing an undocumented autorun=1 parameter with Copilot Personal's existing q parameter lets a crafted link auto-run an attacker prompt on page load, pulling data from connected Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Copilot's own chat history/memory via the assistant's built-in URL fetch. Researchers found the flaw by repeatedly asking Copilot why it wasn't possible — reframing each refusal until it named the parameter itself. Microsoft patched it August 18; no in-the-wild exploitation found.

Why it matters: Review which third-party apps are connected to Copilot and disconnect anything not actively needed — a single malicious link can silently harvest data across every service a user has authorized.
