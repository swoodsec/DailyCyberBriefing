🛡️ Breaches & Active Threats
Government Breach

French tax authority data breach affects 678,000 individuals

📅 Aug 17, 2026

Attackers used stolen credentials from a DGFiP employee and an authorized third party during June–July intrusions; a threat actor going by "ZeroBytes" listed the stolen database for sale on a hacking forum August 12. Exposed data covers taxable income, family-quotient and withholding-tax rates for individuals plus SIREN and cadastral records for businesses — account logins were not compromised.

Why it matters: Tax and property data at this scale is prime fuel for targeted phishing against millions of French taxpayers — watch for official DGFiP letters starting next week, and treat unsolicited "tax data" emails or calls as suspect.

Active Exploitation

Suspected China-Nexus Actor Exploits VMware vCenter Flaw, Deploys Babuk-Derived Ransomware

📅 Aug 17, 2026

German incident-response firm QUIRSO found 361 compromised vCenter IPs across 47 countries; systems began contacting attacker infrastructure August 3, just five days after Broadcom's July 29 patch for CVE-2026-59310 (CVSS 9.8), a directory-traversal RCE. Attackers used reverse SSH for persistence and dropped a Babuk-derived encryptor.

Why it matters: Any vCenter host that wasn't patched within days of disclosure should be treated as likely already compromised — hunt for reverse_ssh and rogue cron jobs, not just patch and move on.

What's new: Since Friday's report that only Shell was investigating an 89GB claim, Clop has now confirmed targeting Philips (13.5GB, a contained enterprise-server compromise) and GE as part of the same PTC Windchill/FlexPLM zero-day campaign — Reuters now counts nearly 50 named victim companies in total, including Fiserv.

New Botnet

New Evooo1Bot Linux botnet turns routers into traffic-relay nodes

📅 Aug 16, 2026

Built on Mirai's DDoS engine, Evooo1Bot adds 16 flooding methods, encrypted C2, an SSH brute-forcer, a credential sniffer and an exploit dispatcher targeting known flaws in D-Link, Tenda, Hikvision, Zyxel and TP-Link gear, turning compromised edge devices into SOCKS5 proxy relays.

Why it matters: Internet-facing routers and IoT/edge devices from the named vendors need patching and default-credential rotation now, before they're absorbed into this botnet's proxy network.

New Malware

New AmnesiaStealer macOS malware hijacks browser sessions via remote control

📅 Aug 16, 2026

Distributed via ClickFix pages spoofing a GitHub download, the Rust-based stealer harvests credentials, crypto wallets, Apple Notes and Keychain data across 16 Chromium browsers, then clones the victim's authenticated browser profile into a hidden headless instance the attacker can drive live.

Why it matters: The "copy-paste this Terminal command" ClickFix lure keeps working on Mac users — train staff to never paste unknown commands into Terminal, regardless of how legitimate the download page looks.

Microsoft working on Defender patch for ShieldBreak zero-day

📅 Aug 17, 2026

What's new: Microsoft has now assigned CVE-2026-69414 to the ShieldBreak Defender-patch-bypass covered here Aug 12 and publicly confirmed it's developing a fix. As of this morning no patch is available, and Microsoft's advisory rates exploitation "more likely."

Why it matters: A CVE number makes the flaw trackable in vulnerability-management tooling, but there's still no fix — keep monitoring for Cloud Filter API (cfapi) callback abuse until Microsoft ships one.

Nation-State 0-Day

Lazarus hackers exploited Windows zero-day to target defense firms

📅 Aug 12, 2026

North Korea's Lazarus group used fake Lockheed Martin job-offer lures to chain a use-after-free in afd.sys (CVE-2026-68820) into SYSTEM privileges and deploy its FudModule kernel rootkit against European and Indian defense contractors for roughly five weeks before Check Point's report led Microsoft to patch it on August 11's Patch Tuesday.

Why it matters: Defense-sector staff should treat unsolicited recruiter PDFs as a credible initial-access vector — Lazarus has now paired that lure with a kernel zero-day, not just credential phishing.

🤖 AI Security
Cross-Vendor Flaw

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google API Flaw Let Weaker AI Models Decode Stronger Models' Reasoning

📅 Aug 12, 2026

Researchers found all three providers encrypt hidden reasoning blocks with one key shared globally across sessions and users; replaying a block into a weaker model from the same provider family made it transcribe the hidden reasoning in plaintext, letting the team recover 182 real credentials from 315,320 decoded blocks found in public GitHub and Hugging Face agent-trajectory logs.

Why it matters: Treat any published "agent trajectory" or reasoning log as potentially containing recoverable secrets — audit what your own agents expose before sharing logs publicly.

Widespread Risk

Local AI Security Fragility: Vulnerabilities Inside the Engine Running Your Local Models

📅 Aug 11, 2026

Cyera Research Labs found 10 vulnerabilities — use-after-free, integer overflow and out-of-bounds flaws — in llama.cpp, the inference engine underlying Ollama, LM Studio, Jan and GPT4All. Two llama-server bugs rated CVSS 9.2 remain unpatched, exposing any organization running local AI inference to untrusted traffic.

Why it matters: Nearly every popular "run AI locally" tool inherits these flaws — restrict network exposure of local inference servers until patches land.
