Stripe to acquire AI "gateway" OpenRouter for more than $7 billion

📅 Aug 16, 2026

Bloomberg reports payments giant Stripe has finalized a deal to buy OpenRouter — the marketplace that routes requests across 400+ AI models for 8 million users — for over $7 billion, roughly five times the startup's $1.3B valuation from May.

Why it matters: Model-routing and cost optimization have become core enterprise-AI plumbing, and the deal pushes Stripe well beyond payments into the AI infrastructure stack.

📅 Aug 15, 2026

Nvidia's latest 13F shows its ~214.8M-share Intel position (bought at $23.28) has ballooned to about $30B, alongside a newly disclosed $21B stake in SpaceX and a complete sell-off of its Arm holdings.

Why it matters: Nvidia is increasingly acting as a strategic investor shaping the chip and space supply chains its own business depends on.

📅 Aug 14, 2026

After nearly seven months of review, the CPUC cleared Waymo to charge for fully driverless rides across most of California's population — all of the Bay Area and Los Angeles, plus new Sacramento and San Diego markets.
