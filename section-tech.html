Notable

Palantir revenue soars 93% and it hikes guidance yet again

📅 Aug 3, 2026

Palantir reported Q2 revenue of $1.94B, up 93% year over year, and lifted full-year guidance to about $8.15B (from $7.65B), driven by a record $2.13B in U.S. commercial contract value. CEO Alex Karp told CNBC that no business at Palantir's scale "has even grown half this much" and that demand "looks like this is going to go on for at least another 18 months."

Why it matters: Palantir has become the clearest public read on enterprise AI spending translating into signed contracts — its acceleration counters fears that the AI buildout isn't yet producing paying customers.

Microsoft posts its biggest one-day gain since 2008 as Azure tops $100B

📅 Jul 30, 2026

Microsoft shares rose about 15.5% — their largest single-day move since October 2008 — adding roughly $480B in market value after Azure crossed $100B in annual revenue for the first time and Q4 Azure growth of 43% blew past guidance. Nearly every metric beat, with quarterly revenue near $90B and EPS of $4.74 versus a $4.24 consensus.

Why it matters: The rally revived the broader AI trade, signaling that investors will richly reward hyperscalers whose massive capex is visibly converting into cloud revenue — even as they punish those that can't show the payoff.

📅 Jul 29, 2026

Meta beat on revenue ($60.8B, up 28%) but its free cash flow cratered from $8.5B a year ago to just $784M as quarterly capex hit $31.1B. Why it matters: Meta is the flip side of Microsoft's story — proof that the market has stopped giving every hyperscaler a pass, and that runaway AI spending without a clear revenue payoff now gets punished.

Tim Cook warns of a "100-year flood" in memory pricing as Apple raises prices

📅 Jul 30, 2026

On his final earnings call, Cook said Apple "reluctantly raised prices" because of what he called a "100-year flood" on memory pricing, language he'd never used in 40+ years in electronics, and warned it will pay even more for DRAM in the September quarter. He noted the memory market is essentially controlled by three suppliers — Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung.

Why it matters: The AI-driven memory shortage is no longer just a data-center story — it is now visibly inflating the price of phones, PCs, and other consumer devices heading into the holiday cycle.

Google pulls its Google Earth AI image tool one day after launch

📅 Jul 31, 2026

Google rolled back a new Google Earth feature that let users type prompts to generate photorealistic aerial scenes on top of real satellite imagery, after people used it to fabricate a nuclear plant in Iran and a plane crashing into One World Trade Center. Google said it is "rolling back this feature while we work on implementing stronger guardrails."

Why it matters: Bolting generative AI onto an authoritative mapping product blurred the line between real and synthetic geospatial imagery — a cautionary tale as platforms race to add AI to trusted reference tools.

Notable

China's Chang'e 7 arrives at spaceport for a lunar south-pole mission

📅 Aug 1, 2026

China's multi-element Chang'e 7 spacecraft — an orbiter, lander, rover, and a first-of-its-kind hopping probe — is at the Wenchang launch site for final preparations ahead of a Long March 5 liftoff, with a launch window reportedly opening in late August. It will target the illuminated rim of Shackleton crater to hunt for water ice and scout sites for a future research base, carrying 21 payloads including six international instruments.

Why it matters: Chang'e 7 is China's most ambitious lunar mission yet and a direct move in the race with NASA's Artemis program to establish a foothold — and secure water ice — at the resource-rich lunar south pole.

Update

Aug 12 delivers a total solar eclipse by day and a moonless Perseid peak by night

📅 Aug 1, 2026

August's sky is a blockbuster: on August 12 a total solar eclipse traces Russia, Greenland, Iceland, and northern Spain — Europe's first since 1999 — and hours later the Perseids peak under a New Moon, with up to 80–100 meteors per hour in near-perfect dark skies. The next Perseid peak on a moonless night won't come until 2045.

Why it matters: A rare eclipse over mainland Europe plus the year's best meteor display on the same night is a once-in-a-generation event for millions of skywatchers — and a windfall for solar physicists chasing the Sun's corona during totality.

Neutral-atom quantum computers are inching toward being genuinely useful

📅 Jul 28, 2026

IEEE Spectrum reports 2026 is shaping up as the year customers get their hands on early "level-two," error-corrected machines — including a Microsoft and Atom Computing system bound for Denmark — as neutral-atom, silicon-spin, and other approaches all post rapid error-rate improvements. Recent milestones include an 18-qubit silicon processor running autonomously at 4 Kelvin.

Why it matters: After years of lab demos, quantum hardware is crossing from science project toward practical tool, with real error-corrected systems shipping to customers for the first time.
