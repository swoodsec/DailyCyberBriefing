Physicists demonstrate the first universal topological gate set on quantum hardware

📅 Jul 15, 2026

A team from Quantinuum, the University of Chicago, Harvard, and Stony Brook used Quantinuum's H2 trapped-ion processor to entangle 54 qubits into a non-Abelian topological state and then braid and fuse anyons into a complete, universal set of quantum gates — published in Nature. The approach could sidestep the resource-hungry "magic state distillation" that bottlenecks fault-tolerant designs.

Why it matters: Topological encoding is inherently more resistant to noise, and clearing this decades-old theoretical barrier on real hardware opens a potentially cheaper path to error-corrected quantum computing.

NASA's Webb finds a hidden third giant planet in the famous Beta Pictoris system

📅 Jul 22, 2026

Using the James Webb Space Telescope, astronomers directly imaged a new giant planet, Beta Pictoris d, orbiting the young, nearby star already known to host two imaged worlds. That makes Beta Pictoris only the second planetary system with at least three directly imaged planets.

Why it matters: Directly imaging multiple planets around one young star gives researchers a rare live laboratory for how planetary systems — including our own — take shape.
