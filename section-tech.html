Moody's says 'unprecedented' AI spending threatens the credit quality of Amazon, Meta and Alphabet

📅 Jul 24, 2026

Moody's Ratings warned that the race to build AI infrastructure at a roughly trillion-dollar annual clip is eroding free cash flow and raising balance-sheet risk at the hyperscalers, forcing even the world's most cash-rich companies to lean on debt, stock sales, and off-balance-sheet financing. The note landed a day after Alphabet and Tesla posted negative free cash flow and lifted their capex plans, helping wipe hundreds of billions off megacap valuations.

Why it matters: When a major ratings agency flags AI capex as a credit risk, the market's frame shifts from how much these giants can spend to how they will pay for it — a warning that colors the rest of a capex-heavy earnings season.

EU clears the $55B Saudi-led take-private of Electronic Arts

📅 Jul 23, 2026

The European Commission granted antitrust approval to the $55B leveraged buyout of EA by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake, and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners, clearing one of the deal's last big regulatory hurdles. Shareholders are set to receive $210 a share, and the transaction — which would saddle EA with more than $20B in debt — is expected to close in the first quarter of fiscal 2027.

Why it matters: At $55B this would be the largest leveraged buyout ever and puts one of the biggest Western game publishers under sovereign-fund control, a landmark for both the gaming industry and Gulf capital's push into tech.

Starship Flight 13 flies a mostly successful mission and deploys the first V3 Starlinks

📅 Jul 25, 2026

On its 13th test flight, SpaceX's Super Heavy-Starship deployed 20 next-generation V3 Starlink satellites for the first time — the program's long-sought payload milestone — and sent the ship to an intact splashdown in the Indian Ocean, even re-lighting a Raptor in space. The booster took a "hard" splashdown after only five of 13 engines relit, and a same-day GAO report flagged cryogenic fuel transfer as a top risk for the vehicle's Artemis Moon role.

Why it matters: Proving Starship can actually release operational payloads is the gate the program had to clear before it can loft the higher-capacity V3 Starlinks the constellation depends on — and before it can ferry NASA astronauts to the lunar surface.

Wildfires force evacuation of NASA's Deep Space Network station in Spain

📅 Jul 24, 2026

The Madrid Deep Space Communications Complex — one of three sites in NASA's Deep Space Network — was evacuated as wildfires closed in, temporarily disrupting a key node for communicating with interplanetary spacecraft. The DSN's three globally spaced complexes provide continuous coverage for missions across the solar system.

Why it matters: The aging, three-site Deep Space Network is a single point of failure for nearly every deep-space mission, and climate-driven wildfire risk to critical ground infrastructure is a growing operational concern for space agencies.

SAXON Q launches room-temperature diamond quantum computers

📅 Jul 21, 2026

German firm SAXON Q announced commercial availability of its 128- and 512-qubit diamond NV-center systems — said to be the first such machines to exceed 10 qubits — that run at room temperature with no cryogenic cooling or vacuum equipment. A proprietary sulfur co-implantation process reportedly yields greater than 85% qubit conversion and up to 99.92% gate fidelity.

Why it matters: Room-temperature, facility-free quantum hardware could put quantum experimentation within reach of ordinary labs and even mobile settings, sidestepping the costly cryogenics that gate most competing approaches.
