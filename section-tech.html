SpaceX and Tesla to invest $16.8B in "Terafab," billed as the world's largest chip factory

📅 Aug 6, 2026

Governor Greg Abbott announced that SpaceX will build a vertically integrated, ~100-million-square-foot semiconductor plant in Grimes County, Texas, bringing logic, memory, packaging and testing under one roof to make AI chips for use on Earth and in space. Phase one carries a $16.8B investment and 3,000 jobs, backed by a $30M state grant.

Why it matters: If built out, Terafab would be a landmark attempt to onshore and vertically integrate advanced chipmaking — reshaping the AI supply chain and Texas's industrial base.

New Jersey sues Amazon on antitrust grounds over its power over delivery contractors

📅 Aug 4, 2026

NJ Attorney General Jennifer Davenport filed what the state calls the first monopsony-conduct complaint of its kind, alleging Amazon suppresses delivery-driver wages, blocks unionization, and stops delivery firms from hiring each other's drivers. The state seeks treble damages and a permanent injunction.

Why it matters: A novel labor-market antitrust theory against Amazon could open a new front in Big Tech regulation focused on workers, not just consumers or rivals.

SanDisk beat on revenue and earnings but its September-quarter guidance fell short of lofty expectations set by a 469% rally this year, dragging memory and AI-chip peers lower. AMD had also posted record 50%-plus growth yet still fell more than 8% on the week.

NASA astronauts complete a 6.5-hour spacewalk to ready the ISS for new solar arrays

📅 Aug 6, 2026

Jessica Meir and Anil Menon installed the seventh and final set of mounting hardware for new roll-out solar arrays on the station's power channel, in a successful 6.5-hour spacewalk. The arrays will add power for critical operations, including the ISS's eventual controlled deorbit.

Why it matters: Upgrading aging station power is part of the practical groundwork for keeping the ISS running safely through its final years.

NASA sets an Aug 30 launch for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope

📅 Aug 2026

NASA's next flagship observatory, arriving at Kennedy eight months ahead of schedule, is now slated to launch Aug 30 on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy. Roman's field of view is at least 100 times larger than Hubble's and could measure light from a billion galaxies over its lifetime.

Why it matters: Roman is poised to become a major engine of astrophysics discovery, mapping dark energy and exoplanets at a scale Hubble never could.
