🔬 Research & Capabilities
Notable

An unreleased Claude pushes a 37-year-old Riemann-zeta bound from 41.6% to 67.2%

📅 Aug 11, 2026

Anthropic disclosed that a research build of Claude improved a proven lower bound tied to the Riemann Hypothesis over two Claude Code sessions, 60 sub-agents, and 31M tokens. It did not prove the hypothesis, but the machine-verified result — reviewed by external mathematicians — is the biggest single advance on that specific bound in decades.

Why it matters: It is a concrete data point that frontier models can now synthesize scattered specialist literature into genuinely new, checkable math rather than just restating known results.

📅 Aug 2026

Hugging Face's summer landscape review finds open-weight models — led by Chinese releases like Kimi K3, Qwen, DeepSeek and GLM — now trailing the best closed models by a narrowing margin on coding, reasoning and agent benchmarks, even as several labs quietly hold weights back for safety review.

Why it matters: The open/closed capability gap is now measured in weeks, reshaping how enterprises weigh cost, control and data residency against raw frontier performance.

ChatGPT for Mac adds "Computer History" — a searchable log of what you did

📅 Aug 14, 2026

OpenAI's opt-in Computer History feature turns clicks, typing, shortcuts and app-switches on a Mac into searchable "memories" so ChatGPT can recall the last file you edited or summarize your morning. Unlike Microsoft Recall it skips screenshots, but critics flag that it records keystroke-level activity.

Why it matters: Persistent, assistant-readable activity histories make AI far more useful — and turn collection, retention and deletion controls into a new layer of OS-level privacy risk.

Stripe clinches a >$7B deal to acquire model-router OpenRouter

📅 Aug 16, 2026

Payments giant Stripe agreed to buy OpenRouter — a gateway that routes developer requests across 400+ AI models by price and performance — for more than $7B, roughly a 5x markup on the $1.3B valuation it raised at in May. Stripe declined to confirm, so treat it as reported.

Why it matters: A multibillion-dollar price validates neutral model-routing as core AI infrastructure — and plants Stripe between developers and every major model provider.

OpenAI disbands its Preparedness team, folding frontier-risk work into product groups

📅 Aug 17, 2026

OpenAI reportedly dissolved the team that assessed whether its models could enable catastrophic bio or cyber misuse, distributing that work into specialized divisions. Why it matters: How labs govern frontier risk — a dedicated watchdog versus embedded ownership — is becoming as scrutinized by regulators as raw benchmarks.
